# Ashenbell regional model — coordinate-free commitment

## Regional geography

The old bell road is the invariant north-south spine. It leaves a damp, tree-compressed approach, crosses Bellwater once, enters Ashenbell through its productive southern edge, passes the warning plinth, then climbs onto the exposed western shoulder of Split Crown Ridge. The quarry track is an older industrial branch that leaves the pass toward the east. It never substitutes for the northern road.

Split Crown is one landform seen at three scales: a paired silhouette after the South Route compression, a framing ridge above the village road, and two physical cairn stones at the pass. Repeating the *relationship* of two stones is intentional; repeating exact path geometry is not.

## Area adjacency

```text
South Route <-> Ashenbell <-> Highland Pass -> conceptual north
                                      |
                                      +<-> Old Bell Quarry (optional)

Optional unlocked shortcut: Ashenbell <-> Highland Pass
```

All ordinary transitions are paired. Removing the quarry and shortcut leaves the complete mandatory path. The pass itself is the experiment's northern threshold; there is no fifth map.

## Historical facts and knowledge

Immutable facts:

1. The quarry supplied resonant stone bells fitted with metal tongues.
2. Ashenbell relayed warnings of storms and slides to three valleys.
3. A quarry slide ended production and the warning chain decades ago.
4. No one is intentionally operating the chain now.
5. The recent sound is lower than the old warning note and correlates with west wind and water.
6. Quarry evidence supports vibration through cracked wet stone and buried metal fittings, while leaving modest uncertainty.

Knowledge distribution:

- South shepherd: comparison to remembered cadence plus weather correlation; no mechanism claim.
- Ashenbell keeper: authoritative purpose, material, closure, and non-operation.
- Pass surveyor: topographic transmission and wind/runoff correlation.
- Quarry worker: material evidence and plausible mechanism, stated as inference rather than certainty.

No speaker may change the slide date, claim the warning chain still operates, or deny the quarry's bell production.

## Ecological gradient

- South: shybulb-dominant damp shade, squabbit at openings, rare elofly.
- Village edge: squabbit-dominant seed plots and elofly during calm air; no shybulb.
- Pass: elofly-dominant exposed air, fewer rock-sheltering squabbit; no shybulb.
- Quarry: elofly in shafts, shybulb at wet cuts, rare squabbit.

Encounter geometry also changes: one deep side pocket, one small fenced margin, two broken highland shelves, and a dense lower quarry cut plus upper shaft.

## Progression state

`r2_shortcut:open` has one producer: bracing the quarry hoist. It has four consumers: locked/open feedback at the village stair and locked/open feedback at the pass stair. The two open events teleport in opposite directions. The state is monotonic; no action clears it. Ordinary village/pass warps never test it.

`r2_bell_history:read` is produced at the village plinth and consumed at the pass cairn, so an earlier optional interaction gains later context without gating progression.

## Optional discovery

The quarry's lower cut provides a potion and survey notes. Its central crack and buried iron reinterpret the pass tone. The hoist gives a useful traversal consequence. Players who skip it still reach the conceptual north and can return normally.

## Spatial identities and pacing

- South Route: narrow start, lateral pressure, Bellwater crossing, short encounter pocket, paired-ridge reveal, village threshold.
- Ashenbell: south work plots, compact homes facing the road, central civic plinth, northeast climb. Buildings form clusters tied to residence/records/work, not a scatter.
- Highland Pass: short switchbacks between sparse stone shelves, central paired cairn, explicit east branch, open north threshold. It must use fewer plants and more negative space than the south.
- Quarry: lateral entry, dense rock walls, central worked ring, descending investigation loop, terminal hoist. Its density and enclosure contrast with the pass.
